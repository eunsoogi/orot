## Minimum native evidence on the integrated revision

Before allocating a destination, carry these candidate assertions into the focused component or SQLite selection:

- **#688:** `DailyReportResultStates` covers failed regeneration retaining the saved revision and source revision change hiding it. `DailyReportSourceInvalidation` covers immediate removal after cited-source deletion. `dailyReportConversationCancellation` covers no provider work after cancellation and saved-answer delivery when cancellation interrupts the read after commit. Add independent lookup/preview settlement and routine refresh with a known-valid saved revision if those boundaries still share one loading gate.
- **#692:** `ConversationTreatmentAction.sqlite` covers local safety, ordinary composer draft preservation, and verified dispatch without adding raw input to history. `ConversationTreatmentOwnership.sqlite` covers saved provenance, reopen, follow-up and withholding after deletion. Check a deferred source/saved-list rejection and retry or reopen because the candidate list renders an alert without a dedicated retry control. Check failed submit and cancelled close separately because the form is unmounted during checking. Verify that an unavailable original shows recovery and that a public link is launched only from the matching verified result.

The retired symptom journal and Health Review do not get new screens or loaders. The legacy data panel and AI intent aliases above are the remaining reachable boundaries. Empty saved libraries, offline or opaque permission, and a missing selected identity must each be exercised with their own fixture. No progress percentage or synthetic clinical value should be added to fill an unresolved region.

Use synthetic inputs and a controlled request boundary for delay, rejection and cancellation. Reuse current test IDs or accessible names above. A request may be held until the observation is made; avoid wall-clock sleeps or replacing existing `exists` and `isHittable` assertions.
